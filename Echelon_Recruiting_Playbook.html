Talent Acquisition

Sourcing Strategy

TA owns sourcing — hiring managers partner on network activation. Work the tiers in order: warm connections first, then targeted competitor outreach, then active channels. Never skip Tier 1.

01
Network First
Internal mobility · alumni · referrals
The highest-quality pipeline is already in your orbit. Network candidates arrive pre-vetted for culture and move faster through every stage. Every open req starts here.
Ask the hiring manager at kickoff: "Who in your network has this skill set?" — document names before sourcing begins.
Check internal mobility first: confirm no current Echelon employee is ready for a step-up before going external.
Alumni outreach: former Echelon employees who left on good terms are warm candidates — they already know the culture and the work.
Activate the referral program: share the open role with the full team via the biweekly email — every referral link is tracked in ApplicantPro and eligible for the $500/$500 bonus.
02
Competitor Sourcing
Identify & target talent from comparable firms
Look for professionals in similar roles who are 3–7 years in and ready to move up — experienced enough to contribute immediately, not yet locked into leadership. Prioritize candidates with a record of client delivery and real certifications.

⚠️ Never recruit directly from active Echelon clients. If a target works at a company Echelon is currently engaged with, confirm with the Practice DM before any outreach.
03
Active Outreach
LinkedIn Recruiter · Boolean search · direct messaging
Targeted, personalized outreach only — no mass blasts. Lead with what makes Echelon different: the client variety, the culture, the career trajectory. Messages should feel like they were written for that person specifically.
LinkedIn search filters: certification (CISSP, OSCP, CISA, CISM, CrowdStrike), title + current firm, geography, 3–10 yrs experience. Save Boolean strings in the ATS.
Message framework — keep it under 100 words: 1 sentence on who you are → 1 sentence on why this role is specific to them → 1 clear, low-friction ask.
MX sourcing: Target ITESO, Tec de Monterrey, and Iberoamericana alumni + professionals at MX-based cybersecurity consultancies and MSSPs.
Personal brand signal: Prioritize candidates with active LinkedIn presence, published content, or speaking history — this is a cultural expectation at Echelon from Consultant level up.
04
Community & Events
Professional networks · certifying bodies · conferences
Passive candidates deeply embedded in the craft — not job-hunting, but findable. Best for Principal+, Technical Director, and highly specialized offensive or GRC roles where traditional search won't surface the right profiles.
Certifying bodies: ISACA chapters, (ISC)² community, SANS alumni network, GIAC-certified professionals list.
Conferences & events: DEF CON, Black Hat, RSA, local BSides chapters, ISACA chapter meetings — build a presence before you need to hire.
Offensive Security talent: GitHub profiles, HackerOne / Bugcrowd leaderboards, CTF top finishers, open-source tooling contributors.
University pipeline: Activate target school relationships (Thomas College, Duquesne, ITESO, Tec de Monterrey) for near-grad and recent alumni sourcing alongside the internship program.
📌 On inbound: Job postings on LinkedIn, Echelon's careers page, and ApplicantPro generate inbound — but inbound alone will not fill senior roles. Tiers 1–3 must run in parallel on every requisition above Consultant level. Inbound is a supplement, not a strategy.
Detailed Process

Stage-by-Stage Breakdown

Every stage has a clear owner, actions, and SLA where applicable. Nothing moves forward without the prior stage being complete.

01

Sourcing & Initial Qualification

Owner: Recruiter
  • Identify candidates via LinkedIn, ATS, direct outreach, known network, and competitor poaching.
  • Scan for hands-on incident response, consulting ability, and MSSP background — look past the traditional resume.
  • Certifications required only if tied to a specific partner (e.g. CrowdStrike). Do not filter on certs otherwise.
  • Confirm specialization → begin outreach → schedule screen. Log specialization and cert badge URL in candidate database.
02

Screen, Evaluation & Routing

Owner: Recruiter → TA Lead
  • Screen call: hands-on depth, role fit, compensation alignment.
  • Complete scorecard (specialization + depth of experience). Assign grade: A / B / C.
  • TA Lead reviews A/B scorecards and routes to the correct Practice DM.
  • For MX candidates: confirm professional English fluency at screen.
03

Practice DM Review

Owner: Practice DM
  • DM reviews the full submission package (scorecard, resume, and screen notes).
  • Must respond within SLA window: advance to interview or pass with documented notes.
  • Escalate to Dan/Eric only when the role-specific DM requires it.

What to Screen For

Evaluation Criteria

Echelon roles are niche and deep. Standard resume scans miss the mark — here's what actually matters.

Frameworks: NIST, GDPR, ISO

Standard at Consultant level and above. Screen for hands-on application, not familiarity — ask for specific project examples involving these frameworks.

Core Values Fit

"Turning Pro" and "Client Centricity" are Echelon's core values. Candidates must demonstrate ownership, follow-through, and a client-first mindset. Those who can't speak to this are a cultural flag.

Technical vs. Partner Track

Identify early — not every senior candidate wants to manage people. Mis-routing a strong IC into a management track is a retention risk. Ask directly during the screen.

Director+ Business Development

$3M–$5M sales targets are real expectations at Director and Partner level. Screen for pipeline ownership, account expansion history, and BD comfort — not just delivery.

Personal Brand (Consultant+)

LinkedIn activity, thought leadership, and professional networking are expected from Consultant level up. Candidates invisible online may not fit Echelon's culture.

MX English Fluency

Most client-facing Echelon roles require professional English even for MX-based placements. Confirm fluency level explicitly at screen — don't assume.

Rules of Engagement

Key Guardrails

These are non-negotiables. Deviating from any of these creates rework, erodes trust, or violates process.

🚫

Never source before kickoff

Do not begin sourcing before the kickoff meeting is complete — even for roles that feel urgent or familiar.

🚫

No ATS req without headcount approval

Never open a requisition in ApplicantPro without confirmed headcount approval from Dan/Eric/Ethan (TLB) or the Practice DM.

⚠️

Certifications are the exception

Only roles tied to specific partnerships (e.g. CrowdStrike) require a verified cert. A missing certification is not grounds to archive a candidate.

🗂

Track every role separately

With multiple service lines open simultaneously, the Pipeline Tracker must be updated per role, per DM. Nothing gets lumped together.

⚠️

Confirm routing before submitting

Always confirm the service line before routing. Submitting a Risk Advisory candidate to the Security team wastes time and erodes trust with the DM.

🚫

TA does not draft offer letters

Carolina (TLB) drafts. TA provides candidate info only. Dan/Eric review, approve, and send.

🧭

Don't over-burden Dan Desko

Dan sees the most roles but is not the default submission contact. Route to the service-line DM first; escalate to Dan only when required.

Internal Mobility

Echelon Career Paths

Identify which track a senior candidate actually wants during the screen. Mis-routing a strong IC into management is a retention risk.

Consulting Track

Intern → Senior Consultant

Client-facing delivery. 80% down to 70% utilization targets. The entry and mid-career path for most hires.

Technical / IC Track

Principal Consultant → Technical Director

Deep expertise, no people management. Up to $3.5M in managed revenue at the top of the track.

Partner / Leader Track

Manager → Partner

Business-development driven. Director and Partner carry real $3M–$5M sales targets. Must show BD history at screen.
